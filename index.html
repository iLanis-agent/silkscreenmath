Silkscreen math

Every poster run dies in the screen room: a frame that almost fits, ink that runs out at print 27, and no honest time plan. Three small calculators for exactly those moments - exact arithmetic, with every borrowed number labeled.

Open the calculators

The three tools

  • Frame fit - image size in, smallest standard frame out, with orientation and spare margin.
  • Ink estimate - print size, colors and edition size become grams of ink at labeled coverage and waste norms.
  • Run planner - setups, pulls and dry gaps added up into honest wall-clock time.

Labeled norms

8 cm squeegee margin each side; standard frames 25×35, 30×40, 40×50, 50×70 cm; ink coverage ~9 m² per kg (water-based, standard mesh) with 20% waste; 20 s per pull, 15 min setup per screen, 10 min dry gap. These are planning aids from common studio norms - check your own setup.
